# 25 First Date Questions That Actually Spark Conversation

A short, free list of first-date conversation starters — no awkward silence required.

If you want the full decks (first dates, couples, deep questions, funny, friends) on iPhone and iPad, try **[Question Cards](https://questioncards.app)** — free by Otter Media.

## The list

1. What’s something small that always makes your day better?
2. What’s a hobby you secretly want more time for?
3. What’s the best meal you’ve had recently?
4. What’s a place you’d love to show someone for the first time?
5. What’s a song that always puts you in a good mood?
6. What’s a book, show, or podcast you’d recommend without hesitation?
7. What’s a childhood tradition you still think about?
8. What’s something you’re learning right now?
9. What’s a small risk that paid off for you?
10. What’s the most spontaneous thing you’ve done lately?
11. What’s a skill you admire in other people?
12. What’s your go-to comfort food?
13. What’s a city or neighborhood you’d love to explore for a day?
14. What’s a question you wish people asked you more often?
15. What’s something that always makes you laugh?
16. What’s a goal you’re quietly working toward?
17. What’s a weekend ritual you refuse to skip?
18. What’s the best piece of advice you’ve actually used?
19. What’s a memory that still makes you smile?
20. What’s something you’re proudly old-fashioned about?
21. What’s a conversation topic you never get bored of?
22. What’s a place that feels like home (that isn’t your home)?
23. What’s a tiny luxury that feels worth it every time?
24. What’s something you changed your mind about as you got older?
25. What’s one thing you’re looking forward to this month?
